## Research

My research lies at the interface of applied mathematics and quantum chemistry, with a focus on quantum embedding methods for strongly correlated systems.

<div class="research-grid">

<div class="research-card">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">Density-Matrix Embedding Theory</h4>
<p class="research-desc">Development and mathematical analysis of Density-Matrix Embedding Theory (DMET), a quantum embedding method that decomposes large strongly correlated quantum systems into smaller fragment problems coupled to a bath. My work introduces a generalized DMET framework that relaxes conventional constraints and allows for more flexible bath space construction.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">Grassmann Manifold Optimization</h4>
<p class="research-desc">Analysis of the mathematical and numerical structure of optimization problems over the Grassmann manifold arising in quantum embedding methods. This includes identifying conditions under which a non-convex quadratic objective admits a globally optimal solution via a convex relaxation, and designing efficient Riemannian optimization and SCF algorithms.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">Strongly Correlated Quantum Systems</h4>
<p class="research-desc">Study of quantum systems where electron-electron interactions cannot be treated perturbatively. Quantum embedding methods provide a divide-and-conquer strategy to make these problems computationally tractable while retaining the essential physics of strong correlations.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">Electronic Structure Methods</h4>
<p class="research-desc">Development of rigorous mathematical frameworks for electronic structure calculations, combining tools from functional analysis, optimization on manifolds, and numerical linear algebra to design and analyze ab initio methods in quantum chemistry.</p>
</div>
</div>
